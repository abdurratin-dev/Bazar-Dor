#Bazar Dor

Welcome to our market price application. Here, you can easily view the prices of your daily groceries and essentials. You can check today's prices, yesterday's rates, and the costs from the previous month; the app presents price data separately for 12 markets across six categories. Feel free to check the prices and do your shopping with confidence.

Technologies used:</br>
1.Next.js -> Build the UI</br>
2.App router(Next.js) + Handle page navigation</br>
3.Tailwind CSS + Any component library Styling and responsiveness(DaisyUI, Hero UI)</br>
4.TypeScript / JavaScript</br>
5.BetterAuth</br>


Project features:</br>
1.You can check the prices of daily groceries.</br>
2.You can view market price information for today, yesterday, the past week, and the past month.</br>
3.You can check the prices of various commodities across 12 markets in six divisions of the country.</br>
4.You can create an account in this application.</br>
5.This is a user-friendly application.</br>
